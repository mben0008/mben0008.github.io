Tag Definitions

<article>
Represents a self-contained composition in a document, such as a blog post or news story.
<abbr>
Used to define an abbreviation or an acronym, providing a full description in the title attribute.
<figure>
Used to wrap self-contained content, like images or diagrams, often with a caption.
<blockquote>
Indicates that the enclosed text is an extended quotation from another source.
<cite>
Used to provide a reference to a creative work, such as the title of a book or movie.
